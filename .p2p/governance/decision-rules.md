# Decision Rules

## Default Mode

Bootstrap decisions are made by responsible maintainer decision.

## Decision Outcomes

- `accepted`
- `accepted_with_changes`
- `rejected`
- `deferred`
- `split`
- `merged_into_other`
- `superseded`

## Acceptance Criteria

A proposal can be accepted when:

- the problem is clear;
- the intended outcome is explicit;
- the scope is bounded;
- the implementation plan is actionable;
- risks and exclusions are documented;
- the next execution step is clear.

## AI Constraint

AI-generated content can inform proposal, plan, task, and risk analysis, but the recorded decision must be human-approved.
