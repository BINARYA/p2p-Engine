# Code Of Conduct

This project expects professional, respectful collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Keep discussion focused on the project and the technical problem.
- Disagree with ideas directly and constructively.
- Assume public comments may be read by future contributors.
- Respect maintainer decisions on scope, timing, and project direction.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of issues, pull requests, or reviews.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or PRs; or block
participants when behavior harms the project or its contributors.
